## 论文摘要
This paper presents a dynamic model of cosmic evolution that covers the phase
before the Big Bang through a thought experiment — our universe arises from the local decoherence
of a fully entangled tachyon sea. The model is logically self-consistent, elegant, and contains no
singularity. The evolution after the formation of our universe is completely consistent with the
cosmic inflation model. The entire universe exhibits a cyclic, renormalizing evolution process,
naturally unifying relativity and quantum mechanics. In the evolution of tachyons within an isolated
system, the tachyon hypothesis provides a concrete and unified physical picture for string theory
and loop quantum gravity, reflecting a duality under the physical perspective: the internal view
(string theory) is that of a tachyonic field with imaginary mass and high energy; the external view
(loop quantum gravity) is a “geometric envelope” of high curvature, negative energy, and violent
oscillation — namely, excited gravitons. Since the total energy Etotal=0, gravitons and tachyons are
tightly coupled inside space, while externally only changes in geometry and volume are manifested,
with vibrational information encoded on the spatial surface. The model intuitively combines the
physical pictures of string theory (high-dimensional vibrational modes) and loop quantum gravity
(background-independent geometric quantization), pointing toward a more fundamental “quantum
information - geometry” unified theory. It provides a self-consistent physical picture and mechanism
for the following frontier problems: where spacetime comes from, the black hole information
paradox, what gravity is, dark matter, vacuum energy, why the dark energy (cosmological constant)
is small, …
